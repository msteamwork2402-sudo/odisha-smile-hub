# Google Ranking Plan for Implant Treatment Pages

## Goal
Improve the relevant treatment page’s chance of appearing on Google’s first page for India-wide searches, while preserving strong Odisha and Bhubaneswar relevance. First-page rankings cannot be guaranteed, but the work will align each page with the correct search intent and establish measurable progress.

## Current evidence
- Google Search Console reports the homepage as submitted, indexed, crawlable, and using the correct canonical URL.
- Search Console currently has no reported performance rows for the latest available 28-day period, so there is not yet enough data to claim ranking momentum.
- Semrush India estimates:
  - “dental implant cost”: 3,600 searches/month, difficulty 17/100
  - “full mouth dental implants”: 880/month, difficulty 23/100
  - “single tooth implant”: 320/month, difficulty 31/100
  - “multiple dental implants”: 20/month, difficulty 0/100
- Existing implant pages already have unique titles, H1s, canonical URLs, medical/FAQ/breadcrumb structured data, reviewer information, and internal links.

## Changes

### 1. Assign one search intent to each page
- Cost page: “dental implant cost”, “dental implant cost in India”, “dental implant cost in Odisha”, and qualified price-related questions.
- Full-mouth page: “full mouth dental implants”, full-arch treatment, All-on-4/6 comparisons, recovery, maintenance, and cost factors.
- Single-tooth page: “single tooth implant”, “single tooth implant cost in India”, implant-versus-bridge decisions, procedure, recovery, and suitability.
- Multiple-implant page: “multiple dental implants”, implant-supported bridges, treatment choices for several missing teeth, process, and cost factors.
- Keep the remaining implant pages focused on their distinct clinical topics so pages do not compete with one another.
- Use the medically correct phrase “single tooth implant”; mention awkward variants such as “single mouth implant” only where natural language allows, never as misleading primary wording.

### 2. Strengthen page content for India-wide search intent
- Rewrite titles, descriptions, introductory answers, headings, and supporting copy where necessary while preserving the existing design.
- Add concise, answer-first sections for the questions people actually search.
- Expand comparisons, treatment stages, suitability, risks, recovery, maintenance, and decision criteria using responsible clinical wording.
- Add India-wide context without implying clinics outside Bhubaneswar: online consultation is available across India, while examination and treatment take place at CARE Hospital, Bhubaneswar.
- Use the supplied CARE Hospital links as external trust references where contextually appropriate.

### 3. Add clinic-approved cost information
- Add clear cost tables or ranges only after the clinic supplies approved figures for single-tooth, multiple-tooth, full-mouth, All-on-4/6, grafting, sinus lift, consultations, diagnostics, temporary restorations, and final prostheses where applicable.
- Label every figure with what it includes, what can change it, and that final cost follows clinical examination and diagnostic assessment.
- Do not copy competitor prices, invent packages, or make unsupported warranty claims.
- Until figures are supplied, retain accurate cost-factor guidance rather than publishing estimates.

### 4. Improve authority and trust signals
- Link Dr. Sauvik Singha’s reviewer identity to the supplied CARE Hospitals appointment/profile page.
- Link the treatment location to the supplied official CARE Hospitals Bhubaneswar page.
- Keep reviewer, review date, treatment location, medical disclaimer, and references consistent throughout the implant cluster.
- Add claims or equipment details only when confirmed by the clinic or an authoritative source.

### 5. Improve technical and internal SEO
- Refine heading hierarchy inside cards and treatment steps.
- Strengthen contextual links among cost, single-tooth, multiple, full-mouth, All-on-4/6, grafting, sinus lift, maintenance, and consultation content.
- Confirm every indexable page has unique metadata, canonical URL, one H1, useful image alt text, valid structured data, and sitemap inclusion.
- Check mobile layouts, tables, links, forms, page speed basics, crawlability, and broken links.
- Preserve the existing visual system and conversion actions.

### 6. Build sustainable authority beyond page edits
- Create a citation checklist using only the real business name, Bhubaneswar treatment address, phone number, and website.
- Keep Google Business Profile details consistent with the website; do not create city listings where no physical location exists.
- Identify legitimate dental, hospital, professional, and Odisha-local citation/link opportunities. No paid link schemes, fake reviews, duplicate listings, or mass-generated city pages.
- Prepare a publishing roadmap for useful supporting articles only where keyword evidence and clinical expertise justify them.

### 7. Measure progress
- After publishing, request indexing for the priority pages and confirm sitemap processing in Google Search Console.
- Track the four primary keyword groups and their landing pages monthly, including impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and average position.
- Review results after Google has had time to recrawl; improve pages based on actual query data rather than repeatedly changing titles without evidence.

## Verification
- Validate all priority pages on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm metadata, canonical URLs, structured data, internal links, and sitemap entries.
- Confirm no unsupported prices, locations, guarantees, credentials, or outcomes were introduced.
- Recheck Search Console indexing after publication and use ranking data as the baseline for the next improvement cycle.

## Needed from the clinic
Before price tables are published, provide the approved prices/ranges and inclusions. All other on-page and technical improvements can proceed without waiting for those figures.
